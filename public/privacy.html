Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Official privacy policy for AarohCare platforms and mobile applications.

1. Introduction

AarohCare Technologies Pvt Ltd respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our platform and mobile applications.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name, age, and gender
  • Mobile number and OTP verification credentials
  • Selected clinic, hospital, and doctor information
  • OP token, appointment bookings, and queue tracking logs
  • Location details (only when permission is explicitly granted)
  • Lab report linkages and digital health interaction data

3. How We Use Information

We use your data strictly to create and manage your account, process appointment tokens, estimate waiting times, send notifications, provide customer support, and comply with applicable statutory laws. We do not sell your personal data.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, rectify, or request permanent deletion of your personal data and account. To request account deletion, visit our Account Deletion Page or email aarohcare.in@gmail.com.
