Company Overview

Our Health, Our Time

AarohCare Technologies Pvt Ltd is dedicated to eliminating waiting room congestion and transforming how patients experience outpatient (OP) consultations across India.

Our Mission

In modern healthcare, patients frequently lose hours waiting in crowded clinic lobbies without visibility into when they will actually consult their doctor. AarohCare was founded to respect both patient time and clinical workflows. By developing real-time queue telemetry and automated outpatient scheduling, we ensure patients spend zero unnecessary minutes in waiting rooms.

Our platform allows patients to book OP tokens in under 30 seconds from their mobile phones, monitor their live queue position from home, and arrive at the clinic right before their consultation begins.

What We Build

Zero Wait OP Appointments

Smart booking in under 30 seconds with live token telemetry updating queue pacing in real time.

Clinic Management Suite

Multi-counter management, doctor schedule pacing, and patient flow analytics for practice managers.

WhatsApp Lab Integration

Automated dispatch of diagnostic reports, blood investigations, and prescriptions directly to WhatsApp.

Consolidated Health Records

Longitudinal digital health timelines aggregating past visits and medications across participating clinics.
