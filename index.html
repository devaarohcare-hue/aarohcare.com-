AarohCare — Our Health, Our Time: Smart Healthcare & Clinic OP Management

AarohCare streamlines hospital and clinic outpatient (OP) management with smart doctor appointment booking in under 30 seconds, real-time live token tracking, and zero wait times. Operated by AarohCare Technologies Pvt Ltd, our digital health platform connects patients, clinics, hospitals, and diagnostic laboratories into an integrated, efficient healthcare network.

Zero Wait Outpatient (OP) Experience

Traditional clinic visits often mean hours sitting in crowded waiting rooms without knowing when the doctor will call your name. AarohCare replaces static waiting with live queue telemetry. Patients book an OP token in seconds from their phone, track their position in line in real time, and arrive at the clinic precisely when it is their turn to be seen.

Comprehensive Clinic & Hospital Operations Software

Designed hand-in-hand with leading doctors and practice managers, AarohCare's clinic operations suite empowers medical centers with intelligent scheduling, dynamic doctor availability management, multi-counter queue coordination, and automated patient notifications via approved WhatsApp channels. Clinics reduce desk congestion, eliminate missed appointments, and enhance clinical workflow efficiency.

Diagnostic Lab Records & Live Telemetry

AarohCare connects patients directly with diagnostic laboratories. Clinical reports, blood work, and imaging investigations are delivered digitally to patients via WhatsApp and secured in longitudinal health records, eliminating paper clutter and facilitating rapid doctor reviews during follow-up consultations.
